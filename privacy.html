Travel Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Travel Tracker is a private, invite-only web app used by one family to keep track of their own travel — flights, hotels, reservations and related paperwork. It is not a commercial product, it is not offered to the public, and it has no users other than the small number of people the owner has explicitly shared their data with.

The short version. Your data stays in your own Google Drive. There is no server, no database, no account system, no analytics, and no advertising. Nothing is sold, rented, or shared with anyone. You can revoke this app's access at any time and keep every file.

Who can use it

Access is controlled entirely by Google Drive file sharing. Signing in with a Google account grants no access by itself — you can only see data if the owner has shared the underlying Drive files with your account. There is no sign-up, and no way to request access from within the app.

Where your data is stored

The app has no back-end of its own. It is a static page, and everything it records is written straight to files in the signed-in owner's Google Drive:

  • flight-log-data.json — reservations, invoices, and app settings
  • trips-data.json — trips and their events
  • trip-diaries.json — trip write-ups
  • individual files for uploaded documents and photos (boarding passes, confirmations, insurance documents, and similar)

These are ordinary files in a Google Drive account. The owner can read, export, or delete them at any time using Google Drive directly, with or without this app.

Some information is also cached in your browser (via localStorage and IndexedDB) so the app can open quickly and work without a connection. That cache never leaves your device and is cleared by signing out or clearing your browser's site data.

Access requested from your Google Account

drive To read and write the app's own data files above. Broad Drive access is required because several people share one set of files: a file created by the owner has to be readable by the family members it was shared with, which a narrower per-app scope does not allow.
userinfo.email To tell who is signed in, so the owner gets edit rights and everyone else is read-only.
gmail.readonly Requested only for the owner's account. It is used to find travel confirmation emails (private-terminal reservations and receipts, and confirmations forwarded to a dedicated address) and read their details into the app. It is read-only: the app cannot send, modify, or delete any email, and it does not read mail unrelated to these searches.

Other services data may be sent to

Where the app needs something it cannot do itself, it sends the minimum required:

  • Anthropic (Claude API) — trip details, and the contents of documents or confirmation emails you ask it to read, in order to draft itineraries and packing guidance or extract reservations. Sent only when you trigger such a feature.
  • Google Places — place names and addresses, to look up photographs, addresses and phone numbers for hotels and restaurants.
  • Open-Meteo — coordinates and dates only, for weather forecasts. No personal information is included.
  • Cloudflare Workers — small services operated by the owner: one keeps you signed in, and one starts the scheduled document-reading job. Sign-in credentials held there are used solely to obtain access to your own Drive data on your behalf.

These providers process that data under their own privacy policies and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

What this app does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or tracking technology of any kind.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or usage measurement.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of personal data with anyone.
  • No use of your data to train machine-learning models.
  • No access to any Google data beyond the permissions listed above.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Travel Tracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the features described above, is never sold, and is never used for advertising or for training generalised machine-learning models.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept for as long as the owner chooses to keep the files, because it lives in their Drive rather than anywhere controlled by this app. Deleting a file in Google Drive deletes it outright.

You can withdraw this app's access to your Google Account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Doing so stops all access immediately and leaves your files untouched.

Children

The app is a private family tool and is not directed at, or made available to, the general public.

Changes

If the app's handling of data changes, this page is updated with a new date above. Because the app serves a handful of known people, material changes are communicated to them directly.
